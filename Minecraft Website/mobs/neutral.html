Neutral Mobs

Neutral mobs will attack you only when you hurt them! You are best to leave them alone...

Bee

They produce honey. They sting if they or their nest is attacked.

Spider

Neutral mobs that become hostile when light level drops below 7. They drop silk and spider eyes.

Iron Golem

They protect villages or their creator, and will attack you if you're too unpopular.

Polar Bear

Spawn in snow biomes, and are very protective over their cubs.

Passive Mobs

Passive mobs will never attack a player, and can drop useful loot when killed.

Pig

Passive mobs that drop pork chops when killled. They can be bred for food.

Cow

Passive mobs that produce leather, beef, and milk. They can be milked with a bucket.

Sheep

Passive mobs that can be sheared for wool. They drop mutton when killed.

Chicken

Passive mobs that lay eggs and drop raw chicken when killed.

Cod

Fish that swim in lakes and the sea. They can be caught and eaten.

Rabbit

Small mobs that drop rabbit hide and rabbit meat. They're very hard to catch!

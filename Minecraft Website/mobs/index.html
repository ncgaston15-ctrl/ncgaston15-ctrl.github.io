Mobs

Mobs are creatues that spawn in the Minecraft world. They can be passive, neutral, or hostile.

Passive Mobs

Passive mobs will never attack a player, and can drop useful loot when killed.

Passive Mobs

Hostile Mobs

Hostile mobs will attack you on sight! You get great loot if you kill them though.

Hostile Mobs

Neutral Mobs

Neutral mobs will attack you only when you hurt them! You are best to leave them alone...

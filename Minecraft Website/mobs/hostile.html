Hostile Mobs

Hostile mobs will attack you on sight! You get great loot if you kill them though.

Creeper

Hostile mobs that explode when you get to close. When you are 3 blocks away from one, it explodes! (after 1.5 seconds, dealing 49 damage points!) Killing one drops gunpowder.

Zombie

Zombies attack you at night. They burn up in sunlight, and drop nothing but rotten flesh.

Enderman

They teleport and aggressively attack players. They drop ender pearls.
